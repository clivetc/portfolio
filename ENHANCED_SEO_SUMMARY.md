Your portfolio now has **4 types of structured data** (JSON-LD) that will help Google understand and display your information when people search for you!

### 1. **Person Schema** 👤

Tells Google you're a real person with:

- Full name and alternate names
- Job title and description
- Email and contact info
- Current employer (CDL Software)
- Education (Near East University)
- Social media profiles
- Skills and expertise
- Languages spoken

### 2. **Professional Service Schema** 💼

Tells Google you offer services:

- Software development services
- Service types (Web Dev, React, TypeScript, etc.)
- Areas served (UK, International, Remote)
- Price range
- Contact information
- Healthcare technology expertise

### 3. **Website Schema** 🌐

Tells Google about your portfolio:

- Website name and description
- Publisher information
- Language (English)
- Search functionality

### 4. **Breadcrumb Schema** 🗺️

Helps Google understand your site structure:

- Home → Experience → Projects → Contact
- Better navigation in search results
